The purpose of this study was to design and implement a synthetic clinical note for use in the medical record. The design process included: (a.) an assessment of the existing format; (b.) the development of a conceptual model and (c.) implementation. The conceptual model was used to define the design specifications and was used as the basis for the design process. A synthetic clinical notes template was developed that included the following components: patient demographics, diagnosis, medical history, physical examination, laboratory results, medications, allergies, test results, referral and discharge summary. < br / > The template was implemented in an electronic medical record system. A pilot study was conducted to assess the impact of the template on provider documentation.
