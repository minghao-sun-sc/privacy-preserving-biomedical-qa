INTRODUCTION  A synthetic CT scan report is a structured document that contains a combination of text and figures that are standardized, reproducible, and clinically relevant.    AIM  To create a synthetic report for the preoperative evaluation of patients with prostate cancer, and to evaluate its impact on the clinical management.  PARAGRAPH > < SECTION  The synthetic CT report is based on the preoperative CT scan, MRI, and biopsy reports.  < SECTION AND TECHNIQUE  A structured synthetic CT reports was created. The text in the reports was standardized and organized in a structured format. A synthetic report contains a short summary, a summary of the findings, a differential diagnosis, a management plan, a suggested treatment, a follow-up plan, and an explanation of the final diagnosis.  In the clinical evaluation, the reports were compared to each other and to the clinical notes. The reports were also reviewed by a board of urologists and a radiologist. .
