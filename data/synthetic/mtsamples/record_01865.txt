OBJECTIVE  To determine whether a synthetic clinical note (SCN) could be used to replace the narrative clinical note.    METHODS  A SCN was created by merging two existing clinical notes. To test the validity of the SCN, two physicians independently reviewed 2 0 0 randomly selected clinical notes and the corresponding SCNs. The physicians were asked to rate the clinical notes for clarity, comprehensiveness, and clinical usefulness.  Ease of use and time savings were also evaluated.  To test for inter-rater reliability, one physician reviewed 1 0 0 clinical notes twice.  RESULT  The SCN was considered clear in 7 0% of cases, comprehensive in 9 5%, and useful in 8 0%. The SCN had a mean time savings of 3 1% and a mean ease of use score of 7. 7. The inter-observer reliability was 0. 7 3 (κ). .
