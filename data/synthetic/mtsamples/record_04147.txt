INTRODUCTION  The purpose of this paper is to describe the process of developing a synthetic computerized tomography (CT) report that would facilitate the interpretation of CT scans.    METHODS  A synthesis process was used to create a synthetic report. The process consisted of: a) creation of a preliminary report that included the clinical information most relevant to the diagnosis; b) development of an algorithm that would assign a diagnosis to each of the preliminary reports; c) development and use of an editor to remove the unnecessary sections from the preliminary report; and d) construction of a final report that contained only the essential information necessary to make a diagnosis.  RESULTS  A synthetic CT report was developed that would allow for easier interpretation and review of CT images. The final report contained the necessary information to make an accurate diagnosis. The user can choose to use the final report to interpret a CT scan or to select the most appropriate section for the report.  The synthetic reporting process was implemented into the clinical environment in our hospital. .
